# 官方边界与合格线

Use this file only when comparing training feedback with official outcomes.

- Treat `45 / 75` as the usual national comparison baseline: the announced standard is generally 60% of each subject's full score.
- A `42` line may apply only to a separately announced region or policy. Never use it as the default national benchmark.
- Verify the notice applicable to the user's exam year and region before making a current-pass-line claim.
- A public, reproducible item-by-item essay marking rubric and score-labelled answer corpus are not available here. Do not claim a known numerical error between training estimates and official marking.

Verification sources:

- https://www.miit.gov.cn/jgsj/rjj/rcgz/art/2020/art_9697b8faa3894e4ba1b27b641970bc62.html
- The current local examination notice or China Computer Technology Professional Qualification website.
