# 训练评估量表

Use this as a coaching rubric, not a published official marking scheme. Score each dimension from 0 to 5 with evidence from the current draft. Do not turn the sum into an official-looking precise score.

| Dimension | 5 anchor | 3 anchor | 1 anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 扣题与题干覆盖 | Every required point has a visible, relevant section. | Main theme covered; one point is thin. | Generic article or a required point is absent. |
| 架构专业深度 | Explains constraints, alternatives, trade-offs, quality attributes, and decisions. | Names technologies and gives partial rationale. | Lists concepts without architecture reasoning. |
| 项目真实性与角色 | Context, role, evidence, and limits are internally consistent. | Plausible story with generic evidence. | Inflated role, conflicting facts, or invented-looking claims. |
| 问题-方案-结果闭环 | Each key issue has cause, decision, implementation, adjustment, and validation. | Solutions and results exist but links are weak. | Benefits lack a concrete resolution chain. |
| 组织与表达 | Abstract, context, topic sections, reflection, and conclusion are clear. | Readable but repetitive or uneven. | Fragmented, theory-heavy, or hard to scan. |

## Readiness Bands

- `相对稳妥`: all obligations are covered; facts and trade-offs are convincing; no hard risk is present.
- `临界`: theme is clear, but one or two score-sensitive weaknesses remain.
- `高风险`: prompt coverage, project credibility, or architecture reasoning needs structural repair.

## Hard Risks

- A mandatory sub-question is missing: mark `高风险` until repaired.
- Theory-only writing or no project role/practice evidence: mark `高风险`.
- Material project-fact contradictions: set confidence to `低` and repair the fact card first.
- Fixed-template resemblance without prompt-specific facts or decisions: require targeted replacement.
